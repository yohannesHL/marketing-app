# Launchpad Brand Style Guide

## Visual Direction
Launchpad should feel modern, precise, and optimistic. The system should balance startup polish with product clarity so the same primitives can support both product workflows and future marketing storytelling.

## Typography
- Headlines are bold, tightly tracked, and concise.
- Body copy should stay highly readable with comfortable line-height.
- Overlines signal section framing and should be used sparingly.
- Inline text treatments should never overpower primary content.

## Color Intent
- Brand primary communicates product confidence and main actions.
- Brand accent adds energy for highlights, secondary emphasis, and supporting visuals.
- Semantic roles such as success, warning, danger, and info remain separate from brand identity so status styling stays stable during rebrands.
- Light and dark themes must preserve contrast and keep focus states visible.

## Spacing & Surfaces
- Default spacing favors roomy, editorial layouts rather than dense utility dashboards.
- Surfaces use soft radius and subtle elevation to keep hierarchy clear without heavy chrome.
- Dividers and borders should support grouping, not dominate the layout.

## Interaction Principles
- Primary actions are visually obvious and keyboard focus must remain visible.
- Hover, active, and loading states should feel responsive but restrained.
- Empty, loading, and error states are part of the product baseline, not afterthoughts.
- Components should compose cleanly in product screens and future marketing sections without page-specific hardcoding.
